Your Tor obfs4 bridge has been deployed!

It may take up to 2 minutes for Tor to bootstrap and register with the
directory authorities.  Watch progress with:

  kubectl logs -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} \
    statefulset/{{ include "tor-obfs4-bridge.fullname" . }} -f

Once you see "Bootstrapped 100%", retrieve your bridge line with:

  kubectl exec -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} \
    {{ include "tor-obfs4-bridge.fullname" . }}-0 -- get-bridge-line

The output will look like:

  obfs4 <IP>:<PT_PORT> <FINGERPRINT> cert=<...> iat-mode=0

Share this line with censored users or submit it to:
  https://bridges.torproject.org/submit

IMPORTANT: Make sure TCP ports {{ .Values.config.orPort }} (OR) and
{{ .Values.config.ptPort }} (PT) are reachable from the internet.

The bridge identity keys are persisted in /var/lib/tor.  Do NOT delete the
PVC without backing up the data directory first – losing the keys means the
bridge gets a new fingerprint and all users need a new bridge line.
